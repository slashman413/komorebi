What it is

Komorebi (木漏れ日 — sunlight filtering through leaves) is a slow game. There is a mountain, and there is your breath, and a single clock binds them. You climb not by reflex but by rhythm: each held breath steadies your hands, each exhale finds the next hold. An ecology and a soundscape shift as you settle.

Built in Godot 4.3 for the Steam Deck, where the controller pulses gently with the breath curve, and for Windows and Linux. One authoritative timer drives the visuals, audio, and haptics together — so what you see and what you feel never fall out of sync.
