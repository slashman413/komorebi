## About This Game (long)

**Komorebi** — 木漏れ日, the word for sunlight filtering through leaves — is a game about
slowing down.

You climb a mountain in rhythm with your breath. The 4-7-8 pattern (four seconds in, seven
held, eight out) is the heartbeat of the whole world: the orb of light you breathe with, the
procedural soundscape, and — on the Steam Deck and other rumble-capable controllers — a soft
haptic tide beneath your fingers. Because a single authoritative clock drives all three, what
you see and what you feel never fall out of sync.

There are no fail states and nothing racing you. Each held breath steadies your hands; each
long exhale finds the next hold. As you settle, the mountain's ecology and soundscape answer.

**Features**
- A guided 4-7-8 breathing loop woven into every moment of play
- Contemplative climbing — low-pressure, no timers, no death
- Breath-synced haptics tuned for the **Steam Deck** (also great with any controller)
- Perfectly synced visuals, procedural audio, and haptics from one shared clock
- Short, complete sessions you can drop into whenever you need to breathe
