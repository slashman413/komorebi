## One-liner
Breathe with the mountain — a meditative climb paced by 4-7-8 breathing.

## Elevator pitch (50 words)
Komorebi is a slow game where you climb a mountain in rhythm with your breath. The 4-7-8
breathing pattern drives the light, the sound, and — on the Steam Deck — a gentle haptic tide,
all from one shared clock, so what you see and feel never drift apart. No timers. No failure.

## Why it's different
Most "relaxing" games decorate calm; Komorebi is *built* from it. The breathing pattern is not
UI — it is the engine tick every system reads from. That single-clock design is the game's
technical and emotional core: sight, sound, and touch move as one breath.

## Key features
- Guided 4-7-8 breathing at the center of play
- Contemplative, failure-free climbing
- Breath-synced Steam Deck haptics
- One authoritative clock → visuals, audio, haptics never desync
